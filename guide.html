Controls

Desktop

Move: WASD or Arrow Keys

Aim: Mouse cursor

Fire: Hold Left Click or press Enter

Dock / Undock: E

Pause / Settings: Esc

Mobile

Move: Left joystick

Aim / Fire: Right joystick auto-fires while you aim

Dock / Undock: Exit button while docked

Pause / Settings: Pause button in the top right

Gamepad

Move: Left stick

Aim: Right stick

Fire: Right trigger

Dock / Undock: Y / North button

Pause / Settings: Select

Progression Loop

Rings

Flying through a live ring gives you 100 score, 3 XP, and a short movement boost. Rings re-arm after a delay, so clean routes pay off.

Cash

Destroying enemies and breaking asteroids can award cash. Cash fuels station upgrades and forge module purchases during a run.

Scaling

Enemy pressure ramps with your level. Harder enemy tiers gain more spawn weight over time, so early XP routes speed up both your power and the danger.

Enemies

Arrow

Fast pursuit ship that accelerates into ramming attacks. It is the most direct threat and gets dangerous fast in groups.

Zoid

Mid-range gunship that tries to hold distance and fires parallel shot spreads at you.

Kite

Long-range sniper that charges before firing a larger, harder-hitting shot. Break line and change angle when you see it set up.

Power Ups

Shield

Turns on temporary damage immunity for five seconds. It blocks hits, but it does not stop black holes from pulling your ship around.

Health Pack

Restores 50 health, up to your current max hull.

Safe Zones

Station

Dock to open the classic upgrade menu. Stations heal you gradually, defend their zone, and only have a limited amount of repair material before they run dry.

Forge

Docking opens the forge menu. Its pulse field fully heals you on first entry, damages enemies in the zone, breaks asteroids, and shrinks black holes.

One Purchase Per Forge Visit

After you buy a module at a forge, it kicks you back into space and starts drifting away. Pick your forge timing carefully.

One or More Purchases per Station Visit

After you buy an upgrade or multiple upgrades at a station, it kicks you back into space and starts drifting away. Pick your station timing carefully.

Hazards & Tips

Black holes pull players gently and enemies much harder. They can clear pressure for you if enemies drift across them, but direct contact still swallows ships.

Asteroids damage both players and enemies, then break into chunks.

Rings first, shopping second is usually the cleanest early route. Fast XP is strong, but it also speeds up the enemy pool.

Dock on purpose. Stations are for steady recovery and stat upgrades. Forges are for module spikes and will eject you after a purchase.
